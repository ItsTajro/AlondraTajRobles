01 — About

Stories matter most when people can see themselves in them.

I'm a multimedia storyteller interested in the people, communities, and experiences that often exist outside the center of a camera frame.

My work moves across journalism, communications, marketing, social media, photography, video, broadcasting, podcasting, and interactive storytelling.

At UC Berkeley, I've developed stories exploring community, identity, housing, labor, culture, and representation while also producing campaigns, content, and communications for organizations across digital platforms.

My professional experience also extends beyond media production into project coordination, administrative operations, reporting, audience engagement, records management, and cross-team collaboration.

Whether I'm conducting an interview, building an interactive story, producing a marketing campaign, managing a deadline-driven project, working behind a broadcast camera, or standing behind a podium, my goal remains the same: I want to make people feel something.
